Simple Chicken Korma Recipe
This is a famous Pakistani dish. It has a thick, grainy sauce and a very nice smell.
Ingredients:

  1. Chicken: 750g
  2. Onions: 2 large (sliced)
  3. Yogurt: 1 cup
  4. Oil: ½ cup
  5. Ginger & Garlic Paste: 1.5 spoons
Basic Spices:
Red chili powder, Coriander powder, Cumin powder, and Salt.

Special Touch:

A little Nutmeg/Mace powder and a few drops of rose water or Kewra water.

How to Cook
  1. Fry Onions: Fry the sliced onions in oil until they are golden brown. Take them out and let them get crunchy.
  2. Fry Chicken: In the same oil, put the chicken, ginger-garlic paste, and whole spices. Fry for 10 minutes until the chicken turns white/brown.
  3. Add Spices: Put in the chili powder, coriander, cumin, and salt. (Do not use turmeric). Fry for 5 minutes.
  4. Cook Chicken: Add 1 glass of water. Cover the pot and cook until the chicken is soft.
  5. Make the Sauce: Blend the fried onions with the yogurt. Pour this mix into the chicken. This makes the sauce thick and grainy.
  6. Final Step: Add the nutmeg powder, almonds, and Kewra water. Let it simmer for 2 minutes.
    7. Serve hot with naan or rice. Enjoy your delicious Chicken Korma!

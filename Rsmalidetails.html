Rsmali Recipe

Here is the recipe written in simple English:

1. Make the Sweet Milk

  1. Boil the Milk: Put 4 cups (1 liter) of full-cream milk in a pot and bring it to a boil.
  2. Flavor: Add a pinch of saffron, 5 to 6 green cardamoms, 6 tablespoons of sugar, and 1.5 tablespoons each of chopped pistachios and almonds.
  3. Thicken: Cook on low heat for 12 to 15 minutes, stirring sometimes, until the milk shrinks to about half its size. Take it off the heat and save it for later.

2. Make the Cheese Balls

  1. Boil: Bring 6 cups (1.5 liters) of full-cream milk to a boil. Take it off the heat and let it cool slightly for 5 to 10 minutes.
  2. Curdle: Mix 3 to 4 tablespoons of lemon juice with a 1/4 cup of water. Slowly stir this into the milk until it separates into cheese and watery liquid.
  3. Wash: Pour the mixture through a clean cloth to catch the cheese. Rinse the cheese under tap water to wash away the sour lemon taste.
  4. Knead: Squeeze out the water and let the cheese hang in the cloth for 10 minutes to dry. Rub and mash the cheese with your hands for 10 minutes until smooth. Mix in 2 teaspoons of cornflour and knead for 5 more minutes.
  5. Shape: Roll the cheese into small, smooth, flat balls. Make sure there are no cracks. This makes about 13 to 14 balls.

3. Cook and Put It Together

  1. Sugar Water: Boil 4 cups (1 liter) of water with 1 cup of sugar in a wide pan.
  2. Cook: Drop the cheese balls into the boiling sugar water. Cover and cook on high heat for 15 minutes until they double in size.
  3. Ice Bath: Turn off the heat and put ice cubes right into the pan. Let them sit for 8 to 10 minutes to help the balls keep their shape.
  4. Finish: Gently squeeze the sweet water out of the balls between two spoons. Put the balls into a serving dish, pour the thick sweet milk over them, and decorate with extra nuts.

Serve:
Put it in the fridge for 3 to 4 hours to cool before eating.

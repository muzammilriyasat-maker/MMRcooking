Aloo Methi Recipe

Simple Aloo Methi Recipe
This is a popular Pakistani dish. It has a flavorful taste and a delightful aroma.
Basic Ingredients:

  1. Potatoes: 500g (peeled and cubed)
  2. Methi (Fenugreek leaves): 200g (washed and chopped)
  3. Onions: 1 large (sliced)
  4. Tomatoes: 2 medium (chopped)
  5. Green chilies: 2-3 (sliced)
  6. Oil: ¼ cup
  7. Ginger & Garlic Paste: 1 spoon
Basic Spices:
Red chili powder, Turmeric powder, Coriander powder, Cumin seeds, and Salt.

Special Touch:

A pinch of Garam Masala and a few drops of lemon juice.

How to Cook
  1. Fry Onions: Heat oil in a pan and fry the sliced onions until golden brown. Remove them and set aside.
  2. Sauté Spices: In the same oil, add cumin seeds, ginger-garlic paste, and whole spices. Sauté for a minute.
  3. Add Potatoes: Add the cubed potatoes and fry for 5-7 minutes until they start to soften.
  4. Add Tomatoes & Spices: Add chopped tomatoes, red chili powder, turmeric powder, coriander powder, and salt. Cook for another 5 minutes.
  5. Add Methi: Add the chopped methi leaves and green chilies. Mix well and cook for 10-15 minutes until the potatoes are fully cooked.
  6. Final Touch: Sprinkle garam masala and a few drops of lemon juice before serving.
    7. Serve hot with roti. Enjoy your delicious Aloo Methi!

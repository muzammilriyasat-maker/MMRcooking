Gajer Halwa Recipe

This is a simple recipe for Gajer Halwa, a popular Pakistani dessert.

1. Ingredients:

  1. Carrots: 1 kg (Choose fresh, bright red, and sweet carrots)
  2. Sugar: A little less than $\frac{1}{2}$ cup (adjust to your taste)
  3. Ghee: 4 tablespoons (Preferably desi ghee/clarified butter)
  4. Green Cardamoms: 2 to 3 whole pods
  5. Milk: $1.5$ to 2 liters (to make fresh Khoya / condensed milk solids)
  6. Almonds: As needed (peeled)
2. Steps to Prepare:
  1. Grate the Carrots: Wash and peel the carrots. Use the thicker side of a grater to grate them, as this gives the halwa a better texture.
  2. Cook the Carrots: Place the grated carrots in a pan over high heat. The carrots will release their natural water. Cook and stir until most of this water dries up.
  3. Add Sugar: Pour in the sugar. Stir well and continue cooking over high heat as the sugar melts and the remaining moisture evaporates.
  4. Add Ghee & Cardamom: Add the ghee to the carrots to give them a nice shine and rich flavor. Throw in the green cardamom pods for aroma and mix well until the carrots are completely dry.
  5. Make Fresh Khoya: In a separate pan, pour the milk and boil it over high heat. Keep stirring until it reduces heavily into a thick, paste-like fresh Khoya.
  6. Combine Everything: Toss the peeled almonds into the carrot mixture. Pour the freshly prepared Khoya directly over the carrots.
  7. Final Mix: Cook and stir everything together for about 2 minutes so the flavors blend perfectly.

White Korma Recipe

Here is the recipe for White Chicken Karahi written in simple English:

Ingredients:

  1. Chicken: 1 kg (cut into medium-large pieces)
  2. Oil: 1.5 cups
  3. Black Cardamom: 2 pieces
  4. Ginger-Garlic Paste: 1 tablespoon
  5. Yogurt: 1 cup (250 grams)
Spices
  1. : 1 tablespoon coriander powder and 1 teaspoon cumin powder (both roasted and ground)
  2. Salt: 1 teaspoon
  3. Green Chilies: 3 to 4 (sliced)
  4. Fresh Ginger: 1-inch piece (cut into thin strips)
  5. Dairy Cream: 1 pack (200 to 250 grams)
  6. Black Pepper: ½ teaspoon
  7. Coconut Powder: 1 tablespoon
  8. Lemon: 1 large lemon
  9. Fresh Coriander: A small handful (chopped)
How to Cook:
  1. Fry the Chicken: Heat the oil in a pan. Add the 2 black cardamoms and the chicken. Fry the chicken for 12 to 13 minutes.
  2. Add Ginger-Garlic: Add the ginger-garlic paste to the pan. Fry everything together for another 5 to 7 minutes to remove any raw smell.
  3. Add Yogurt and Spices: Mix the coriander powder and cumin powder into the yogurt. Pour this yogurt mixture and the salt into the pan. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes until the water from the yogurt dries up.
  4. Add Fresh Aromatics: Stir in the sliced green chilies and ginger strips.
  5. Add the Cream: Pour in the dairy cream. Add the black pepper now (adding it at the end keeps the curry perfectly white). Cook on high heat for just a minute. Do not overcook, or the cream might spoil.
  6. Final Touch: Stir in the coconut powder and squeeze the juice of one large lemon into the pan.
  7. Garnish: Turn off the heat, sprinkle fresh coriander on top, and serve!
Serve hot with rice or naan.

Maser Chawl

Ingredients & Preparation

The Lentils:

  1. Take 2 cups of whole brown lentils (Masoor). Wash them 2 to 3 times until the water becomes completely clear, then leave them to soak in water for about 1 to 1.5 hours until they swell and softed.
  2. Put the soaked lentils into a pressure cooker.
  3. Water Ratio:
    If you are serving with rice, use a 1:5 ratio (for 2 cups of lentils, add 10 cups of water). Note: If you plan to eat it with flatbread (roti), reduce the water to 8 cups so it becomes thicker.
  4. Add the crushed garlic (about 2–3 tablespoons), followed by 1 tbsp salt (adjust later), 1 tbsp red chili powder, 1 tbsp coriander powder, ½ tbsp cumin powder, and ¼ tbsp turmeric.
  5. Close the pressure cooker and let it cook for 13 to 14 minutes after it begins whistling.
  6. Open the cooker safely once the steam releases. Turn the stove back on to medium heat and gently mash the lentils with a wooden spoon to give them a creamy texture.
  7. Flavor Option:Soak 5–6 tamarind pieces in a few tablespoons of water. Add 2 tbsp of this tamarind pulp to give the lentils a traditional sour taste. (Alternatively, if you don't want it sour, add ¼ cup of milk instead to make it smooth).Let it simmer for 10 minutes.
The Tempering (Tarka)
    1. Heat ½ cup of desi ghee (clarified butter) or oil in a pan. 2. Add 1 finely chopped garlic clove and let it turn golden brown. 3. Add ½ a finely chopped onion and sauté for a minute. 4. Add 1 chopped tomato, 2–3 sliced green chilies, and some fresh coriander. Cook until the tomatoes soften, then pour this hot mixture directly into the lentils and mix.
Serving
  1. The Rice:Cook 1 kg of rice by heating 1 cup of ghee, frying 4 cardamoms, 7–8 cloves, and 1 tbsp cumin seeds. Add water and 2 tbsp of salt, bring to a boil, then add the rice and cook. Once the water dries, cover and steam on low heat (dum) for 20–30 minutes.
  2. The Salad: Mix together chopped onions, tomatoes, and cucumber, then squeeze fresh lemon juice over top.
  3. Serve the hot, creamy lentils poured over the soft rice with a side of fresh salad and mixed pickles.

Mutton Karahi Recipe

Here is the detailed recipe for Mutton Karahi.

Ingredients:

  1. Mutton: ½ kg
  2. Cooking Oil: ½ cup
  3. Black Cardamom: 2 pieces (to remove the meaty smell)

Instructions:

  1. Tomatoes: Large size, cut into halves
  2. Garlic Paste: As required
  3. Green Chilies: Slit open
  4. Salt: 1 tsp (or to taste)
  5. Black Pepper Powder: ½ tsp

Ginger & Fresh Coriander:
Thinly sliced/chopped for garnishing

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Boil and Soften the Meat: In a wok or pot, add ½ kg mutton, ½ cup cooking oil, salt, and enough water to cook the meat. Add 2 black cardamoms as well. Cover and cook for about 30 minutes until the mutton is completely tender. (If using a pressure cooker, 12 minutes is enough).
  2. Steam the Tomatoes: Place the tomato halves face-down on top of the cooked meat. Cover and let them steam on low heat. Once they become soft, carefully remove and discard the tomato skins using tongs. Mash the softened tomatoes completely into the meat.
  3. Fry (Bhunai) & Add Garlic: Add the garlic paste and green chilies. Turn up the heat and fry the mixture very well. The authentic recipe requires frying until the water dries up completely, leaving a thick, rich tomato-based gravy.

The Final Touch:
Just before turning off the stove, add ½ tsp of black pepper powder. Stir well, and then finish by garnishing with freshly sliced ginger and chopped coriander leaves.

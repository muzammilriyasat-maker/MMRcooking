MMR Cooking

Khree Recipe

Ingredients Needed:

Rice: 1/2 cup (broken rice works best)
Milk: 1 liter (full-fat milk)
Desi Ghee (Clarified Butter): 1 tablespoon
Dry Fruits: Almonds, pistachios, and cashews (chopped), and 1 tablespoon of raisins
Jam-e-Shirin or Rooh Afza: 1/2 tablespoon (gives flavor and a thick texture)
Sugar: 1/2cup (or to taste)

Step-by-Step Instructions:
1 Prepare the Rice

Take 1/2 cup of rice. Grind it coarsely in a mixer (do not make it into a fine powder; keep it grainy).
Wash the ground rice thoroughly and soak it in clean water for about 30 minutes.
After 30 minutes, strain the water and set the rice aside.

2. Boil the Milk

Pour a tiny bit of water into your cooking pot and swirl it around before adding the milk (this stops the milk from burning at the bottom).
Add 1 liter of full-fat milk to the pot and bring it to a boil.

3. Roast the Nuts


In a separate small pan, heat 1 tablespoon of desi ghee.
Add the chopped almonds, pistachios, and cashews. Roast them gently on low heat until they are fragrant. Set them aside. (Do not roast the raisins).

4. Make the Secret Paste

Take 2 tablespoons of your soaked, ground rice and put it into a small blender.
Add 1/2 tablespoon of Jam-e-Shirin (or Rooh Afza) and 1/2 tablespoon of chopped cashews.
Blend this together to make a smooth paste. This paste helps thicken the kheer quickly and gives it a great taste.

5. Cook the Kheer

Once the milk in the main pot starts boiling, lower the heat to medium-low.
Add the rest of the soaked rice, the roasted nuts, and the secret paste you just blended.
Cook this mixture on medium-low heat for about 5 minutes. Keep stirring gently so the milk cream (malai)
mixes back into the pudding. The rice will swell up and the pudding will start to thicken.

6. Add Raisins and Sugar

After 5 minutes, add 1 tablespoon of raisins and 1/2 cup of sugar.
The sugar will release some water, so cook the mixture for another 2 to 3 minutes until it thickens up beautifully again.

7. Cool and Serve

Turn off the stove. The total cooking time should only be around 10 minutes. Keep in mind that the kheer will get even thicker as it cools down.
Pour the kheer into a serving bowl (traditional clay dishes look best).
Garnish the top with some leftover chopped almonds, pistachios, and cashews.
Let it cool down to room temperature, then place it in the refrigerator for 1 hour to chill completely before serving.
